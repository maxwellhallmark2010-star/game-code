## Crumple Zone (`crumple.html`)
A soft-body driving sandbox. Each car is a lattice of mass nodes joined by beams that flex, bend permanently and snap, simulated at 1000 Hz, so crashes crumple the body where it was hit. Includes suspension that bends or tears off, engine damage, shattering glass, sparks, tire smoke, skid marks, ramps, hills, a crash-test wall, slow motion and a beam (node) debug view.

Open `crumple.html` in a browser. W/S drive, A/D steer, Space handbrake, G crash test, T slow-mo, B beam view, right-drag to grab a car. Press H in-game for all controls.
