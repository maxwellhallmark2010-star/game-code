## Crumple Zone (`crumple.html`)
A soft-body driving sandbox. Each car is a lattice of mass nodes joined by beams that flex, bend permanently and snap, simulated at 1000 Hz, so crashes crumple the body where it was hit. Includes suspension that bends or tears off, engine damage, shattering glass, sparks, tire smoke, skid marks, ramps, hills, a crash-test wall, slow motion and a beam (node) debug view.

Open `crumple.html` in a browser. W/S drive, A/D steer, Space handbrake, G crash test, T slow-mo, B beam view, right-drag to grab a car. Press H in-game for all controls.

## Gym Rat (`hoops.html`)
A solo basketball shooting game in an indoor gym, playable in first or third person. Create your player (position with ratings and training points, height, skin tone, hair, beard, gear) and design the jersey (name, number, front text, font, lettering size and colors). Customize the gym: length, width, ceiling height, wood type, gloss finish, board direction, paint, lines, logo, walls, bleachers and lighting. Walk up to the ball rack to grab a ball, dribble, and shoot with a timing meter. Shots fly with real drag and backspin, bounce off a physical rim and backboard, and drop through a cloth net. In crosshair aim mode the ball goes exactly where the crosshair points; auto-aim targets the nearest rim. Includes a session shot chart.

Open `hoops.html` in a browser. WASD move, Shift sprint, hold left mouse (or E) and release at the top of the meter to shoot, Q crossover, Space jump, V switch camera, R rack the balls, M menu.
