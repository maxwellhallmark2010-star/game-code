## Saltwater (`saltwater.html`)
An original open-city sandbox in one HTML file (three.js). Open it in a desktop browser and click to play.

- A coastal island city: downtown towers, neighbourhoods, parks, beaches, and a pier with a ferris wheel
- Walk, sprint, and jump, or steal any car (6 kinds, including police cruisers) and drive with drifting and crash damage
- Traffic that follows lanes and turns at intersections, plus pedestrians who walk the sidewalks, cross streets and run from trouble
- Fists, pistol, SMG and shotgun with aiming, reloading and headshots
- A 5-level heat system: patrol cars chase you along the roads, cops get out to cuff you or shoot, and a helicopter joins at level 4. Break line of sight to lose them
- 4 jobs (car delivery, checkpoint race, gang fight, store heist), cash/health/armor/weapon pickups
- Day/night cycle with lit windows and street lamps, a rotating minimap and a full city map (M)
- 3 procedurally generated radio stations (Q in a car), engine, tyre, siren and gunshot sounds
- Touch controls on phones and tablets

Controls: WASD move/drive, mouse look, Shift sprint, Space jump/handbrake, F or E enter/exit car,
left click shoot, right click aim, 1-4 or mouse wheel weapons, R reload, Q radio, H horn, M map.
