How to play

Aim and shoot. Click and drag on the table (or drag your finger) to aim. The cue stays where you leave it. After a scratch the cue is hidden: drag the cue ball where you want it, and the cue comes back when you let go. Pull the power bar down and release to shoot. Click on the spin ball (right side) for top spin, backspin or side spin; double-click it to reset. Arrow keys fine-tune the aim, Up/Down set the power and Space shoots.

The break. The table starts open. Balls potted on the break do not give you a group, and if you pot one you keep shooting.

Choosing your group. On an open table, the first ball you legally pot after the break decides it: potting a solid (1-7) makes you solids, a stripe (9-15) makes you stripes. Your opponent gets the other group.

Keep going. If you legally pot a ball of your group, you shoot again. If not, it's your opponent's turn.

Fouls give your opponent ball in hand (they place the cue ball anywhere). A foul is: potting the cue ball, hitting nothing, hitting your opponent's ball or the 8 ball first, or no ball touching a cushion after the first hit (unless a ball is potted). After a foul on the break, the cue ball must go behind the head string.

The 8 ball. Pot all seven of your balls, then pot the 8 ball in a pocket you call. The marked pocket is the one you've called; tap another pocket to change it.

You win by potting the 8 ball in your called pocket. You lose if you pot it early, in the wrong pocket, or with a foul. If the 8 ball goes in on the break, the balls are re-racked.
